Help us test it on real work.

We are looking for developers who use Claude Code and regularly need to check whether a generated change satisfies the original request.

If you opt in, we measure how much correction and review your own tasks need with and without Quarterback, and share the results with you. Metrics are opt-in, tied to your account (not anonymous), deletable by you, and we publish nothing without your say-so.

Quarterback runs on your machine; the Claude Code agent sends your task and the code it reads to Anthropic under your account. Data flows.
