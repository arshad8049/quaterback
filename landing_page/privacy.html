Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This policy describes how Velora ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the Quarterback website at quaterback.velorallc.workers.dev and the Quarterback software runtime.

What we collect

When you submit the beta access form, we collect:

  • Your email address
  • The AI coding agent you use most (optional free-text field)

Form submissions are processed and stored using Cloudflare D1 (serverless SQLite hosted in Cloudflare's network). We store your email, optional agent preference, submission timestamp, and IP address.

What we do not collect

  • We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising
  • We do not run third-party analytics scripts on this site
  • We do not sell or share your information with advertisers
  • The Quarterback software runtime is local-first — your source code never leaves your machine during the beta

How we use your information

Email addresses submitted through the beta form are used solely to contact you about the Quarterback private beta. We will not send unsolicited marketing email. You can opt out at any time by replying to any email from us.

Data hosting

This website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Form data is stored in Cloudflare D1. Cloudflare operates globally; data may be processed in the United States and other regions. By submitting the form you consent to your data being processed under Cloudflare's terms.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you. Email ashaik8.us@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Third-party services

  • Cloudflare — hosting (Pages) and form data storage (D1). Cloudflare privacy policy
  • Resend — transactional email delivery (beta signup confirmation). Resend privacy policy
  • GitHub API — the star count shown in the nav is fetched client-side from api.github.com. No personal data is sent; this is a public, unauthenticated read.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product evolves. Material changes will be noted with an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
